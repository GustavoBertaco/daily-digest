## Daniel Pink

**[What Car Salesmen Will Never Tell You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qtldgTar11o)**
Daniel Pink draws on research by Leaf Van Boven and Tom Gilovich to argue that optimizing for material efficiency crowds out the experiences that actually compound into lasting happiness — and that adaptation, not price, is the key variable.

- **Experiences beat material purchases, reliably.** Van Boven and Gilovich asked people to compare purchases over $100 made for an experience vs. a material object; experiences won on both happiness and perceived value for money.
- **Adaptation is the mechanism.** A jacket becomes furniture in your closet within a month. A trip becomes a story — material goods depreciate in hedonic value, experiences appreciate.
- **Over-optimizing your life is its own failure mode.** If you apply efficiency thinking to every decision, you can engineer yourself into a life where nothing surprising or memorable happens.

## AI Engineer

**[Building ambitious software — Jonathan Kelley, Dioxus Labs & Cognition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7vFrcNWXzs)**
Jonathan Kelley, founder of Dioxus Labs, reflects on what happened when his team maxed out coding agent subscriptions to accelerate a Rust cross-platform app framework — and why tens of thousands of AI-generated lines of code failed to clear the bar for merging.

- **The "slop cannon" failure mode.** Kelley coined the term for what happens when teams mistake AI output volume for engineering progress. The generated lines sat in draft; almost none shipped.
- **Quality gates matter more, not less, with AI assistance.** When generation speed exceeds review capacity, the bottleneck shifts to judgment — requiring engineers with high standards, not just more prompts.
- **Ambitious software still requires a prior thesis.** Kelley started the framework the summer before an internship he never took; the ambition predated the tooling. AI accelerates execution of a vision; it doesn't provide one.
- **The subscription trap.** There is a pattern of pouring resources into generation without investing equally in the pull toward quality — review, architecture, and clear acceptance criteria.
