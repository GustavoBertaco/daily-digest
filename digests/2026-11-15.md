## The Knowledge Project Podcast

**[Elon's No-Commission Motivation Trick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCqUDSEA7kE)**
Jon McNeill, former Tesla president and Lyft COO, explains how Tesla built a high-performing sales culture without commissions by applying Charlie Munger's core principle: to understand how a system works, follow the incentives.

- **Removing commissions forced a rethink of what actually motivates people.** McNeill's team couldn't rely on money, so they had to identify what salespeople would genuinely compete for — and found that visible standing worked.
- **A leaderboard in every store replaced financial incentives.** One ranking board per location, with a line drawn in the middle, changed behavior without a dollar of commission attached.
- **The threshold was deliberately arbitrary.** The cutoff wasn't positioned through data or analysis — its power came entirely from visibility and the human aversion to sitting on the wrong side of a public ranking.
