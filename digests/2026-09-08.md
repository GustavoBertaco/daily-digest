## Daniel Pink

**[3 Types of Hobbies](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EVcVtss82MI)**
Relying on a single hobby to restore energy after work tends to fall short because the four mechanisms of recovery — mental detachment, relaxation, mastering something new, and control over your own time — are distinct and do not substitute for each other.

- **Recovery is multi-dimensional, not monolithic.** Researcher Sabine Sonnentag at the University of Mannheim identified four separate restoration pathways: detaching mentally, relaxing, building a new skill, and exercising autonomy over your time. Each pathway delivers something the others do not.
- **Your favorite hobby is probably covering only one mechanism.** A relaxing hobby gives you no mastery; a hobby that builds mastery often keeps the mind engaged and prevents mental detachment.
- **A portfolio of hobby types outperforms optimizing for one.** The research case is for deliberate variety — choosing activities across the four mechanisms rather than defaulting to a single preferred activity.
