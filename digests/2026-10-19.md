## Anthropic News

**[Investing in the public benefit](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund)**
Anthropic is committing a portion of its equity to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a new entity structured to increase its stake as the company grows. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic's shares will be committed immediately, with an additional 2% added at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in company value above a set baseline.
