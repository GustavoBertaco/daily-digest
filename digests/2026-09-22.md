**[Inside Petal: Building the World's First Petabit-Class Transoceanic Subsea Cable](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/09/21/connectivity/petal-petabit-transoceanic-subsea-cable/)**
Petal will be the first subsea cable to deliver petabit capacity at transoceanic distances, spanning approximately 7,000 km between France and the United States, with service expected in 2029. It will be the first subsea system to deploy multi-core fiber technology at scale, doubling capacity per fiber without a proportional increase in power or physical infrastructure, built in partnership with NEC and Sumitomo Electric.
