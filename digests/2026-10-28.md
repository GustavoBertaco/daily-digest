## AWS What's New

**[Amazon ECR now supports replication backfill for existing container images](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/replication-backfill-amazon-ecr)**
ECR's replication backfill lets you replicate your full existing image catalog to configured destination registries, not just images pushed after replication was enabled. Previously, backfilling required manually retagging each preexisting image to trigger replication.

**[OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra is now generally available on Amazon Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/openai-gpt-6-1-terra-on-amazon-bedrock/)**
GPT-6.1 Terra is now GA on Bedrock, extending the GPT-6 family with a balance of capability, speed, and cost for everyday workloads. Compared to GPT-5.6 Terra, it improves visual reasoning, computer use, and factuality, and scores 15 percentage points higher on OpenAI's Last Exam evaluation.

**[Amazon WorkSpaces Core Managed Instances now supports ModifyVolume API](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/amazon-workspaces-cmi-modifyvolume/)**
WorkSpaces Core Managed Instances now support in-place volume changes — size up to 16,384 GiB, type, IOPS, or throughput — without detaching or restarting. Previously, any storage or performance change required rebuilding the instance from scratch.

**[Amazon SageMaker Unified Studio adds light mode support for IDC-based domains](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/smus-light-mode)**
SageMaker Unified Studio now offers a light mode theme for Identity Center (IDC)-based domains, joining the already-supported IAM-based domains. Users can switch between dark and light themes to match their preference and working environment.

**[Amazon Connect Customer expands testing and simulation to validate flow action outcomes](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/connect-customer-validate-flow-actions/)**
Amazon Connect now lets you verify the data returned by flow actions — Lambda responses, Lex resolved intents, Agentic CX variables — during testing and simulation, not just confirm that an action started. When a test fails, you can pinpoint the exact action with an unexpected result rather than tracing back from a downstream step.

**[Amazon Connect Customer now lets you view metrics by customer lifetime value and repeat-caller status](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/connect-customer-boma-segment/)**
Amazon Connect now segments contact-center metrics by customer lifetime value (LTV) and repeat-caller status, surfacing how KPIs differ for high-value or repeat contacts before issues show up in aggregates. Where a segment underperforms, you can route those customers to stronger agents or adjust the knowledge bases and self-service flows they hit.

## AI Engineer

**[What a $900M Bank Error Teaches Us About AI Agents - John Feminella, Two Sigma](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iniryju23Sk)**
In 2020, Citibank sent $900 million to Revlon's lenders despite a warning pop-up and three approvers signing off — the procedure itself was flawed. Two Sigma's John Feminella uses this case to motivate composable controls for AI agents: an agent registry, risk-based review, least-privilege tool access following an age-of-authority model, and others that give agents real autonomy without requiring blanket lockdowns.

**[Why Are We Still Governing AI Agents Like Software? - Tim Tyler, Gravitee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h8jgniNJcbQ)**
Most companies deploy AI agents with no identity, no named manager, and shared credentials — governance that would never be acceptable for a human employee. Gravitee's Tim Tyler presents the Agent Accountability framework, which governs agents like a digital workforce across three stages: Assignment (identity, team, human sponsor), Alignment (role, mission, least-privilege authority), and Administration (oversight scorecards and step-by-step recourse when agents deviate).

**[300 Agents, 60 People: How We Secured Our Own AI Agents - Aiden Sochowski, Runlayer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BgLdvF7Qkh8)**
Runlayer (60 people, 300+ agents daily) put a gateway in front of everything and logged every session and tool call. What observability exposed: prompt injection via email, PR-review agents manipulated into approvals, over-permissioned bots, agent cross-contamination from one poisoned agent, buggy MCP servers burning tokens, and a model switch that raised costs over 100x in a week. None of it was visible before the gateway.

**[How a Trading Firm Ships New AI Models the Same Day - Noyan Tokgozoglu, Optiver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aGIUg8zKI8A)**
At Optiver, a change rolled out to traders shows up in P&L within hours, so AI infrastructure must move fast without sacrificing measurability. Head of AI Engineering Noyan Tokgozoglu walks through their on-prem platform: an inference gateway for routing and budgets, same-day frontier model adoption benchmarked on internal golden sets, and a model-and-harness-agnostic meta harness that kept AI spend from spiraling after a summer of major model releases.

**[Agents That Self Improve While You Sleep - Fuad Ali, Arize AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGuNt2_Oz8s)**
Arize AI's Fuad Ali demonstrates a self-improvement loop for AI agents: trace everything, let an always-on Signal agent read traces on a schedule and file deduplicated issues, convert failures into evals and living regression datasets, run experiments to prove a fix works, and keep humans at the highest-leverage points — eval design, dataset curation, and fix review. The approach keeps the loop autonomous while ensuring human judgment shapes what counts as correct.

**[How to Test Your Agents on Real Tasks With Harbor - Xander Song, Arize AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kRJjSOYqW0I)**
Harbor is an open-source framework from Arize AI for evaluating agents on realistic tasks in isolated Docker sandboxes with real data. Engineer Xander Song explains its primitives — tasks, verifiers, oracles, and ATIF trajectories — then shows Harbor at scale on Daytona, where a prompt change directing agents to a new SQL tool produced a measurable cut in total tool calls.
